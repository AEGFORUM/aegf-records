## Constitutional Principle

> "AEGF does not claim authority over the field. It creates visibility for the people building within it."
> — Adopted as the constitutional principle of the forum. Attributed to Saad Muzafar.

---

## What AEGF Is

- A neutral forum for visibility, dialogue, and ecosystem mapping
- A public Registry where every solution is listed in its own words
- Attribution guaranteed — every contribution attributed permanently to its source
- Governed by 75% supermajority — no individual holds veto authority
- Async-first — global participation across all time zones

## What AEGF Is Not

- A standards body
- A certification programme
- A trade association or representative body
- A commercial vehicle for any member
- A consensus machine

---

## Governance Transition Chain

Attributed to Tim Zlomke, Intellectual Framework Lead:

**Evidence → Trust → Authority → Permission → Action → Consequence**

---

## Constitutional Governance Rules

**75% Supermajority Rule** — Proposed by Frank Schouten. All constitutional changes, declarations, recommendations, and representative statements require 75% member approval. No individual holds veto authority.

**Attribution Guarantee** — No listing, contribution, or attributed output goes live without explicit written approval of the exact final text from the contributor. Established as governing precedent by Syeda Beenish Fatima.

**Four-Phase Discipline** — Session architecture by Saad Muzafar. Individual contributions first, observed themes second, no forced consensus, no recommendations until Phase 4 if members choose.

**IP Protection** — Participation transfers no intellectual property, ownership, exclusivity, or commercial rights. Every member's work remains entirely their own.
