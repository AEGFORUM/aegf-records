### How this works

Every role above carries a standing, ongoing mandate — not a one-time contribution. Each role-holder is expected to produce at least one visible deliverable per month, posted to this record. This keeps the Forum's structure real rather than ceremonial, and keeps the operational load distributed rather than resting on any single person.

> "AEGF does not claim authority over the field. It creates visibility for the people building within it."
